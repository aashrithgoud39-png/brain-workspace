    Questions people ask first.

    How do I get an account?

    Managers create profiles. Ask your manager for your ID and a starting password, then sign in.

    Where do my documents go?

    Uploaded files are stored in your own Brain database. The search model runs on the same server, so documents are not sent to an outside AI service.

    Can an employee open a restricted document?

    No. Restricted text is filtered out inside the search query. An employee only sees how many results were hidden.

    Which files can I add?

    PDF, Word, CSV, plain text and Markdown, up to 5 MB each. Managers upload them and choose who can find them.

    Does Brain write answers for me?

    Not yet. It returns the best matching passages with their source, so you can read the original. Written answers come later.
